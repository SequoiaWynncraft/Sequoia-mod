## Sequoia 1.12.0

- Add a **quest board** for Leaf, Sequoia's guild currency. Run `/seq quests` or select **Quests** in the sidebar to see your level, XP, and Leaf, your daily, weekly, and repeatable quests with what each pays, and your recent Leaf. See [Quests and Leaf](https://github.com/SequoiaWynncraft/Sequoia-mod/blob/main/docs/players/quests.md).
- Add a **profile card** at the foot of the Sequoia sidebar with your head, name, rank, insignia, level, Leaf, and XP bar. Click it to open the quest board.
- Celebrate Leaf as it arrives with pixel-art popups: a toast for a raid or war, a card for a finished quest or milestone, and a ceremony for the biggest milestones and level-ups. Ceremonies wait until you are out of a raid or war.
- Show defending Sequoia's claim as **Defended** and **Defence**: those wars pay twice as much Leaf per war point.
- Show the Leaf each level-up pays, on its ceremony and in your recent Leaf.
- Built on Java 21.0.13.1, which fixes CVE-2026-114938. Update your launcher's Java 21 to 21.0.13.1 or later.
