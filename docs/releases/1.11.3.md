## Sequoia 1.11.3

- Add a **Show queue timers** setting for the war queue HUD, on by default. Queue timers now stay visible while you are unavailable for wars or have no queue of your own, and showing them never marks you available. Turning it off hides only the HUD; waypoints and reminders for your own queues keep working.
- Keep valid queue timers when Sequoia rejects a queue report or a leave request.
- Clear queue timers when you log out of Sequoia or lose guild access, instead of showing stale ones.
