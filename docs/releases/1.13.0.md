## Sequoia 1.13.0

- Replace the Repeatable quest with a **Timed** quest. A new one opens every four hours, at 00:00, 04:00, 08:00 UTC and so on, and stays open for two hours. Everyone in the guild gets the same one, and it pays more than a daily. Between windows the card says when the next one opens. Repeatable quests you had open are gone; the ones you finished still count.
- Show both ways to finish a quest, such as **Complete 3 NOL raids or 9 wars**. Each goal has its own picture and bar, and whichever you finish first completes the quest. The popup and the Recent list name the goal that finished it.
- Stop a raid profile refresh from undoing your own save, or a live update, that arrived while it ran.
- Make Esc on Settings, Connection and Party Finder go back to the screen you came from, like every other Sequoia screen, instead of closing to the game.
- After the ingredient guide fails to load, wait a minute before asking again, and then ask again by itself, instead of asking every time you open it.
- Update the sandbox that decodes chat image previews to fix a critical security bug on Apple Silicon Macs and ARM Linux.
