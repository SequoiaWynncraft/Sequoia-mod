## Sequoia 1.12.1

- Stop disconnecting you over ordinary Sequoia errors. They now show once in chat, and the connection stays up.
- Stop reconnecting and signing in about once a second when Sequoia refuses this mod version or a party finder ban applies. You get one message, and the mod waits until you run `/seq connect`.
- Keep you signed out after `/seq logout` or Clear session until you run `/seq connect`. Switching to another Minecraft account still signs that account in.
- Make the Disconnect button on the Connection screen disconnect.
- Keep reconnects to one connection at a time, and stop a failed reconnect from ending automatic reconnection for the rest of the session.
- Forget the previous account's party in the party finder when you switch accounts. Promote, Kick, Join and Leave now always act on the party and player you clicked.
- Send guild storage rewards before switching accounts, instead of dropping them.
- Stop the previous account's unlock popups from appearing after you switch accounts.
