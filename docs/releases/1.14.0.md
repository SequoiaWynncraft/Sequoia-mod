## Sequoia 1.14.0

- Switching Minecraft account, `/seq logout`, Clear session and a sign-in Sequoia refuses now each end everything that belonged to that sign-in: the party finder, waiting popups, the raid profile sign-in, Princess stats, and the war planner and queue. Nothing from the previous account shows up for the next one.
- Keep your Discord name and a skipped raid profile setup per Minecraft account.
- Close the War Planner and raid profile setup when you switch account, instead of acting for the new one.
- Show "Connected as <name>" once you are signed in, and actually retry after "Retrying in 2s".
- Wait longer between sign-in attempts after repeated refusals, even across reconnects, instead of starting over each time.
- Show the update notice when Sequoia needs a newer mod version at connect time.
- Party Finder invites, kicks, promotions and Invite All act only for the account and server connection you started them on, and their messages show only there.
- Invite All sends one invite at a time and skips anyone who left or already joined.
- Clicks in Party Finder land on the card or button that was drawn, including the swinging filter chips. Delist and Manage Party act only on the party they were opened for.
- Connect to Sequoia with Java's own WebSocket client instead of a bundled library.
