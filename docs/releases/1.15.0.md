## Sequoia 1.15.0

- Reroll a daily or weekly quest you don't fancy with the ↻ button on its card. Each slot's first reroll of the day is free, and each one after costs 100 Leaf. Point at the button to see the cost, and click it twice to swap. Leaf charges exactly the cost the card showed, or nothing.
- The quest board's Recent list shows your latest Leaf again. It showed the oldest of your last 50 events, often a day old.
- A milestone paid without a tier is drawn as the milestone it paid for, and a balance in debt is shortened like any other.
