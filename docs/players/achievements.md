# Achievements

Run `/seq achievement` or select **Achievements** in the sidebar.
The gallery groups raid, war, profession, role, and raiding-event badges.
Hover a card for details. Progress cards show your current count and the next milestone.

Raiding events have one card with its earned tier or unearned status, without a progress bar.
Wartorn Palace has four tiers, Nexus of Light has three, and The Canyon Colossus has an untiered badge.

The backend supplies counts, tiers, milestones, and event awards.
Progress follows the Minecraft account across machines. Linked alternate accounts retain separate totals.
Recorded raid announcements count from 14 August 2026 at 18:12:02 UTC.

The mod refreshes periodically and shortly after a local completion.
If no cached result exists and the backend is unavailable, the screen reports the failure instead of showing zero completions.

**Announce new Sequoia badges tiers** controls guild-chat badge announcements.
See [announcement behavior](../achievement-announcements.md) for delivery details.
