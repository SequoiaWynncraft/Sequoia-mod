# Quests and Leaf

Leaf is Sequoia's guild currency. Members earn it, and XP, from guild activity. XP adds up to a Sequoia level.
Leaf can't be spent yet.

## Earning Leaf

| Source | Leaf | XP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raid | 1 per point of season rating it earns | As much as the Leaf |
| War, FFA or snipe | 1 per war point you score | As much as the Leaf |
| War over Sequoia's claim | 2 per war point you score | As much as the Leaf |
| Quest | Shown on the quest's card | Shown on the quest's card |
| Guild raid and war milestone | Set for each tier | As much as the Leaf |
| Each level you reach | A tenth of the XP the level took: 100 for level 2, 300 for level 10 | None |

Raids and wars count within about a minute of finishing.

## Quest board

Run `/seq quests`, select **Quests** in the sidebar, or click your profile card at the foot of the sidebar.

The top of the board shows your level, your XP toward the next one, and your Leaf.
Under it are three quests:

- **Daily.** One new daily becomes available each UTC day, and up to three bank up, shown as pips beside the title. Playing every third day loses nothing, and there are no streaks. Finishing one starts the next banked one at once.
- **Weekly.** One per week, Monday to Monday in UTC. It ends with the week.
- **Repeatable.** The next one starts as soon as you finish it.

Each card shows what to do, the Leaf and XP it pays, and how far along you are.

Most quests can be finished by raiding or by warring, such as **Complete 3 NOL raids or 9 wars**.
The card shows both, each picture over its own bar, and whichever you finish first completes the quest.
A war goal asks about three wars for each raid, since a war takes about a third of a raid's time.
Season rating quests count raids and wars together, so they keep one bar.

Under the cards, **Recent** lists the last six things that paid Leaf, finished a quest, or reached a level.

The board refreshes at most once a minute while it is open.
If Leaf can't be reached, it keeps showing what it had and marks it as possibly out of date.

## Profile card

The card at the foot of the Sequoia sidebar shows your head, name, rank, insignia, level, Leaf, and XP bar.
A **!** beside your rank means Leaf couldn't be reached and the balance may be out of date.
The card is left out when the window is too short for it and every sidebar button.
In **Settings** it is also left out where it would hide the Princess mode controls.

## Popups

While you are on Wynncraft and connected to Sequoia, a popup plays the moment something pays:

- **Toast:** Leaf from one raid or war, in the corner. A war over Sequoia's claim says **Defended**.
- **Card:** a finished quest, or a Bronze to Gold milestone.
- **Ceremony:** Platinum and higher milestones, and level-ups, with the Leaf they paid.

A ceremony never covers a raid or a war. It waits until the fight is over.

## Who can use it

Quests and Leaf are for current Sequoia members.
The mod reads Leaf with your Sequoia session and never signs in on its own.
If you are signed out, the board and the card say to connect. Run `/seq connect`, and they update straight away.
